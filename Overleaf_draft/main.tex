\section*{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Model Scale:} Experiments limited to Llama-3.1-8B; scaling behavior to 70B+ models remains untested.
    \item \textbf{Language:} English-only evaluation; cross-lingual transfer unexplored.
    \item \textbf{Instruction Types:} 10 predefined types may not cover all alignment scenarios.
    \item \textbf{Evaluation:} TruthfulQA uses heuristic markers; human evaluation would strengthen claims.
\end{itemize}
