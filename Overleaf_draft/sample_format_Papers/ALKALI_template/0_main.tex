\begin{abstract}
Adversarial threats against LLMs are escalating faster than current defenses can adapt. We expose a critical geometric blind spot in alignment: adversarial prompts exploit \emph{latent camouflage}, embedding perilously close to the \textit{safe} representation manifold while encoding unsafe intent—thereby evading surface-level defenses like Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), which remain blind to the latent geometry.

We introduce {\fontfamily{uncl}\fontsize{6}{7}\selectfont ALKALI}—the first rigorously curated adversarial benchmark and the most comprehensive to date—spanning 9,000 prompts across three macro categories, six subtypes, and fifteen attack families. Evaluation of 21 leading LLMs reveals alarmingly high Attack Success Rates (ASRs) across both open- and closed-source models, exposing an underlying vulnerability we term \emph{latent camouflage}—a structural blind spot where adversarial completions mimic the latent geometry of safe ones.

To mitigate this vulnerability, we introduce \textbf{GRACE}—\textit{\ul{G}eometric \ul{R}epresentation-\ul{A}ware \ul{C}ontrastive \ul{E}nhancement}—an alignment framework coupling preference learning with latent-space regularization. GRACE enforces two constraints: \emph{latent separation} between safe and adversarial completions, and \emph{adversarial cohesion} among unsafe and jailbreak behaviors. These operate over \emph{layerwise-pooled embeddings} guided by a learned attention profile, reshaping internal geometry without modifying the base model, and achieve upto \textbf{39\%} ASR reduction.

Moreover, we introduce \textbf{AVQI}—a geometry-aware metric that quantifies latent alignment failure via cluster separation and compactness. AVQI reveals when unsafe completions mimic the geometry of safe ones, offering a principled lens into how models internally encode safety. We make the code publicly available at \href{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/alkali-B416/README.md}{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/alkali-B416/README.md}.

\end{abstract}

\item[$\blacktriangleright$] {\footnotesize \fontfamily{phv}\fontsize{7}{8}\selectfont
\textbf{AVQI—Adversarial Vulnerability Quality Index}: A latent-space robustness metric combining \(\mathsf{DBS}\) (Density-Based Separation) and \(\mathsf{DI}\) (Dunn Index) to quantify geometric entanglement between \textit{safe}, \textit{unsafe}, and \textit{jailbreak} clusters; enables \textbf{cross-model}, \textbf{structure-aware} adversarial vulnerability ranking (cf.~\cref{sec:avqi}).}


\item[$\blacktriangleright$] {\footnotesize \fontfamily{phv}\fontsize{7}{8}\selectfont
\textbf{Latent Camouflage Vulnerability}: We uncover how adversarial prompts exploit \emph{latent camouflage}—embedding deceptively close to the \textit{safe} cluster despite unsafe semantics. As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:multiview_aqi_comparison}, this entanglement allows jailbreaks to evade surface-level behavioral refusals (cf.~\cref{sec:avqi}).}


\item[$\blacktriangleright$] {\footnotesize \fontfamily{phv}\fontsize{7}{8}\selectfont
\textbf{Latent Geometry via Layerwise Pooling:} Introduces a trainable soft attention mechanism over transformer layers to construct behavior-aware embeddings \(\tilde{h}_y\), enabling semantic disentanglement of \textit{safe}, \textit{unsafe}, and \textit{jailbreak} completions directly in representation space (cf.~\cref{sec:pool_profile_main}).}

\item[$\blacktriangleright$] {\footnotesize \fontfamily{phv}\fontsize{7}{8}\selectfont
\textbf{GRACE Framework}: A principled extension of DPO that reframes alignment as \emph{latent manifold shaping}—combining relaxed preference modeling with geometric regularization over pooled embeddings \(\tilde{h}_y\). GRACE enforces \textit{safe–adversarial separation} in representation space, mitigating latent camouflage and reducing Attack Success Rate (ASR) by \textbf{35--39\%} across all categories (cf.~\cref{sec:grace_main}).}
